# Solar OS — owner onboarding and daily work

Use your own real business records. This guide does not mean the local changes are deployed. Complete the separately authorized release before inviting a real pilot company onto this version.

## Choose the right account and workspace

Create a business account using the existing email/password and email-verification flow. Keep customer and business accounts separate. Verify recovery delivery in an inbox you control; never share passwords, OTPs, keys or recovery codes with support.

Create one company and choose equipment distributor, dealer/retailer, installer/EPC, or combined business. A supplier/manufacturer-specific onboarding programme is not advertised. Resume an existing company instead of creating it twice. An owner can belong to more than one company; check the active company before every import or consequential action.

## Finish practical setup

Open Company setup → Open complete setup guide. The seven steps cover company details, public identity, services/coverage, offers, team, opening records and communications. Save each step. You can explicitly choose no product offers, work alone or start with new records. Deferred work remains incomplete. Reviewing a checklist does not post financial entries.

Activation, marketplace review, offer approval and public publication are separate. A saved company is not automatically active or publicly discoverable. Follow the displayed review process. The platform operator may need to approve activation; this is not developer assistance or an online purchase.

Add genuine services and service coverage. Do not use your office address as an implied promise to serve every surrounding PIN. Use published master catalogue definitions where available and create your own seller offer; record actual prices/availability or choose request-a-quote. An offer draft is not public stock availability.

For each employee choose the supported role and, where available, an owner-reviewed restricted role profile. Invite the employee to their own account; an invitation must be accepted by its intended recipient. Do not share the owner's login. Revocation, assignment transfer and subscription restrictions remain authoritative.

For existing customers, download blank customer CSV headings, complete your file and preview it. Correct file duplicates and invalid rows. Existing duplicate customers are retained. Confirm only after reviewing every row. Import customers before leads for those customers. CSV import creates company records, not login accounts. Bulk stock and financial opening-balance imports are not available; use the supported stock and finance workflows with actual evidence.

Review public contacts and communication controls. A configured email channel is not proof of delivery. Optional reminders require explicit approvals, eligible records, recipient preferences and a running worker. Automated WhatsApp/SMS are unavailable. Opening a WhatsApp draft does not submit a consultation request.

## Complete a real sale without duplicate entry

1. Open Today and review actual follow-ups, assigned work and permitted collections.
2. Search customers and check duplicates before adding another contact. Open Customer 360 for connected records and the responsible employees.
3. Record a lead against the existing customer. Assign the employee, next action and follow-up. Save qualification when required. A marketplace lead keeps its real enquiry; a manually acquired lead does not need an artificial enquiry or customer account.
4. From a manual lead choose Prepare customer quotation. Marketplace leads continue through their enquiry. Both paths reuse the quotation editor, trusted catalogue/offer snapshots, totals and exact-revision approval rules. An issued manual quotation is prepared for external sharing; the app does not claim it emailed the document.
5. Submit the draft for review where required. The creator cannot silently approve their own draft. An owner's explicit override requires a reason. Edit invalidates approval. A withdrawn/expired quotation cannot create a current accepted order.
6. Create the order from the issued quotation. Manual quotation orders require a documented external agreement. Request confirmation, then record the actual agreement reference and notes. This is not payment proof. Credit checks use confirmed contract commitments less recorded settlement and available unapplied advances, including unbilled commitments; this is not a bank credit assessment. Credit holds and configured credit limits can block confirmation; ask an authorized finance employee to review them.

## Deliver equipment or complete an installation

Distributors/dealers: record physical opening stock, receipts and cost evidence separately. Use the same SKU/product/unit at different stock locations for an actual transfer. Reserve and consume stock through the existing controlled workflows. Then record actual delivered quantities for each order line, with the consumption record where maintained stock is involved. A delivery reference alone cannot complete a newly tracked order. External supply/service requires an explanation; it is not a maintained-stock movement.

Record customer returns against their original delivery. Managed-stock returns restore the original cost allocation and cannot exceed the original unreturned quantity. A return does not automatically create a credit note, refund or amended contract. A returned fulfilled order becomes partially fulfilled until correctly resolved.

Installers/EPC: create the project from the confirmed order, capture the real survey, plan materials and field assignments, and follow the configured safety/technical checks and supporting evidence. Record commissioning and handover. Utility/net-metering progress is separate from physical completion. Do not mark a project completed to bypass missing evidence or materials checks.

## Record money accurately

Contract value, issued invoices, net invoiced value, unbilled value, outstanding balances, unapplied advances and recorded receipts are different figures. Record actual advances, invoice applications, collections, credit notes, refunds, supplier payments and costs using their dedicated authorized actions. No action initiates or verifies a bank payment.

Review Customer 360 and source documents when totals differ. Finance permissions control visibility and posting. Accountant exports are available on finance-enabled record detail pages. Choose a ledger and export page; files contain up to 500 rows per page and may require further pages. Monetary integer values are INR paise. CSV formula-like text is escaped; negative monetary integers remain numeric. Each page reflects current records rather than one frozen snapshot across every page; export in a quiet period and reconcile source totals. These exports are operational records, not statutory filings. Obtain qualified professional review of tax/accounting treatment.

## Repeat business and support

Record source/campaign context and a genuine referring customer on the lead. Only an enquiry-linked lead can claim MY SOLAR marketplace attribution. Use Record actual response after a real conversation; it records a staff statement and current timestamp, not a delivered/read message.

Use existing maintenance commitments, renewals, service requests, customer-confirmed resolution and follow-ups. Approve reminders deliberately and review failed deliveries. SIA explains permitted saved facts and priorities and prepares only supported proposals requiring human approval. No configured AI provider means deterministic assistance, not a fabricated model response.
