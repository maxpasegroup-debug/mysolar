# Solar OS — packaging, delivery and controlled launch

## Verified code rules

There is no hardcoded purchasable FREE/ELITE/PRO price catalogue. Plans, versions, prices, entitlements and limits are configured and snapshotted by the existing commercial service. The reserved code `pro` specifically controls PRO partner-network and part-time-sales-executive access; display names alone cannot grant those features. FREE and ELITE must be validated against actual authorized plan records, not assumed from marketing labels. No plan names or agreed historical terms were changed here.

Pre-billing mode and assigned subscription access remain distinct. Manual activation is not verified payment. Expiry/downgrade preserves authorized existing data and blocks restricted new actions. Read the effective access mode and current plan from Plan & access status. Do not advertise checkout, automatic recurring billing, payment collection or provider reconciliation as implemented.

Company roles and configured entitlements intersect. A plan does not turn a salesperson into an accountant. Business presets tailor presentation and suggestions; they do not grant role access or publish a company.

Live production plan configuration, commercial viability and operating costs were not inspected in this local-only phase. Authorized operators must review those records and actual pilot usage before publishing final offers or prices. Do not substitute local fictional test plans for commercial evidence.

## Repeatable delivery package

Each supported company receives the same shared application/backend, its saved preset, reviewed public identity and genuine service coverage, role-controlled team access, the owner guide, and the practical setup/onboarding checklist. Preserve MY SOLAR marketplace identity separately from distributor branding.

For a branded platform, reuse the existing draft/publish website workflow. Agree content, support scope and commercial terms first. The domain foundation verifies ownership and records lifecycle/provider evidence, but the shipped domain provider is explicitly unconfigured. No live provider adapter, external resource, DNS record or certificate provisioning was added. Do not promise one-click custom-domain delivery.

The owner delivery checklist is at `/#/distributor/setup?step=delivery`. It shows actual setup/access/publication information and external gates; it is not release approval. A public share link appears only when the current marketplace view confirms eligibility.

## Controlled pilot acceptance matrix

Use only separately approved participants. The owner will onboard real companies; this task creates none. At least one representative of each advertised preset must demonstrate its advertised workflow before broad expansion:

| Preset | Required pilot demonstration |
| --- | --- |
| Equipment distributor | Company setup, catalogue/offer, employee invitation, manual/customer-enquiry sale, stock receipt/transfer/reservation, partial delivery/return, invoice and collection |
| Dealer/retailer | Duplicate customer review, counter/manual sale, quotation approval, documented agreement, delivery, credit controls and after-sales request |
| Installer/EPC | Qualified lead, scoped quotation/order, survey, schedule, materials, assigned mobile task/evidence, commissioning/handover and utility progress tracked separately |
| Combined business | Both stock/sales and installation workflows with reconciled customer, order, project and financial records |

For each scenario record the release identifier, company/account role, action, expected/actual result, safe evidence reference, issue and resolution. Include an unfamiliar owner and employee, mobile use, permission denial, recovery email receipt and the customer's service experience. Do not invent success counts or silently populate a pilot company.
